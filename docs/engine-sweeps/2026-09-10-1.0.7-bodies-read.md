# The 1.0.7 body changes, read (2026-09-10)

**What this is.** The second half of the P10a sweep `docs/engine-sweeps/2026-09-09-{server,client}-0.221.12-vs-1.0.7.md`:
every surface member whose BODY changed between 0.221.12 and 1.0.7, read against the recorded fact it
carries, one verdict each. The eight a visit rides on were read on 2026-09-09 (the sweep header); the
other 25 on the server axis and the 2 more the client axis carries were read here. **Every one holds.**
Two are worth knowing about beyond "holds", marked ★.
